    <p>The forms of <a class="popup-hyperlink" data-bs-toggle="popover" data-bs-html="true" data-bs-title="Explore Further" data-bs-content="<a href='/Sonic-Unreal/esports'>Esports</a>" style="text-decoration: underline; font-weight: bold;">esports</a>, <a class="popup-hyperlink" data-bs-toggle="popover" data-bs-html="true" data-bs-title="Explore Further" data-bs-content="<a href='/Sonic-Unreal/nightlife'>Nightlife</a>" style="text-decoration: underline; font-weight: bold;">nightlife</a>, and <a class="popup-hyperlink" data-bs-toggle="popover" data-bs-html="true" data-bs-title="Explore Further" data-bs-content="<a href='/Sonic-Unreal/virtual-worlds'>Virtual Worlds</a>" style="text-decoration: underline; font-weight: bold;">virtual worlds</a> that appear in this project share a set of common foundations. Material connections abound: <a class="popup-hyperlink" data-bs-toggle="popover" data-bs-html="true" data-bs-title="Explore Further" data-bs-content="<a href='/Sonic-Unreal/the-story-of-a-song'>The Story of a Song</a>" style="text-decoration: underline; font-weight: bold;">the same song being used at a nightclub and during a virtual concert featuring esports characters</a>, <a class="popup-hyperlink" data-bs-toggle="popover" data-bs-html="true" data-bs-title="Explore Further" data-bs-content="<a href='/Sonic-Unreal/lil-nas-x'>Lil Nas X</a>" style="text-decoration: underline; font-weight: bold;">the same musician performing a virtual concert and designing an esports character</a>, <a class="popup-hyperlink" data-bs-toggle="popover" data-bs-html="true" data-bs-title="Explore Further" data-bs-content="<a href='/Sonic-Unreal/esports-and-nightlife'>Esports & Nightlife</a>" style="text-decoration: underline; font-weight: bold;">the same jersey being worn by an esports player and a go go dancer</a>.</p>
    <p>Yet more important than these material connections are the shared conceptual frameworks that tie together these three scenes. Nightlife, virtual worlds, and esports - in how they manifest in this project - are best understood as a network of aesthetic, discursive, technological, and interpersonal relations rather than as separate case studies. The most important unifying characteristic of this network for our purposes is its <a class="popup-hyperlink" data-bs-toggle="popover" data-bs-html="true" data-bs-title="Explore Further" data-bs-content="<a href='/Sonic-Unreal/the-nature-of-unreality'>The Nature of (Un)Reality</a>" style="text-decoration: underline; font-weight: bold;">ambivalent relationship with reality</a>, wherein each of these cultural practices is popularly understood as offering an escape from <a class="popup-hyperlink" data-bs-toggle="popover" data-bs-html="true" data-bs-title="Explore Further" data-bs-content='<a href="/Sonic-Unreal/what-is-reality">What is "Reality"?</a>' style="text-decoration: underline; font-weight: bold;">the “real world.”</a> This ambivalent relationship with reality is negotiated through ideas of <a class="popup-hyperlink" data-bs-toggle="popover" data-bs-html="true" data-bs-title="Explore Further" data-bs-content="<a href='/Sonic-Unreal/liveness'>Liveness</a>" style="text-decoration: underline; font-weight: bold;"><em>liveness</em></a>, <a class="popup-hyperlink" data-bs-toggle="popover" data-bs-html="true" data-bs-title="Explore Further" data-bs-content="<a href='/Sonic-Unreal/virtuality'>Virtuality</a>" style="text-decoration: underline; font-weight: bold;"><em>virtuality</em></a>, and <a class="popup-hyperlink" data-bs-toggle="popover" data-bs-html="true" data-bs-title="Explore Further" data-bs-content="<a href='/Sonic-Unreal/immersion'>Immersion</a>" style="text-decoration: underline; font-weight: bold;"><em>immersion</em></a>.</p>
    <p>“The Sonic Unreal” argues not that esports, nightlife, and virtual worlds are worthy of study because they depend upon these shared foundations; rather, it argues that these shared foundations <em>themselves</em> are worthy of study and can most easily be understood through their varied manifestations in these three interconnected communities of practice.</p>
