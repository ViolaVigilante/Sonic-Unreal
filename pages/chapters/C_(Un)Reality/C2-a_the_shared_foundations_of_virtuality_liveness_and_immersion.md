    <p>“The Sonic Unreal” argues that <a class="popup-hyperlink" data-bs-toggle="popover" data-bs-html="true" data-bs-title="Explore Further" data-bs-content="<a href='/Sonic-Unreal/the-nature-of-unreality'>The Nature of (Un)Reality</a>" style="text-decoration: underline; font-weight: bold;">the contested borderlands between reality and unreality</a> are a primary location where politics are performed in <a class="popup-comment" data-bs-toggle="popover" data-bs-html="true" data-bs-title='The Digital Age?' data-bs-content='By “the digital age,” I am referring not to some notion that all people’s lives are uniformly saturated by digital technology in the ways often assumed by many US academics, but rather to shifting structures of global politics and capital which are increasingly reducing life to modes of digital computation.<br><br>For more on digital governance, see:<br><br><a target="_blank" href="https://mitpress.mit.edu/9780262029575/the-stack/">Bratton, Benjamin H. 2016. <em>The Stack: On Software and Sovereignty</em>. Cambridge, MA: MIT Press.</a><br><br><a target="_target" href="https://www.upress.umn.edu/9781517917418/machine-and-sovereignty/">Hui, Yuk. 2024. <em>Machine and Sovereignty: For a Planetary Thinking</em>. Minneapolis: University of Minnesota Press.</a>'>the digital age<sup><b>&#8224;</b></sup></a>. (Un)reality is a difficult topic to study, however, <a class="popup-hyperlink" data-bs-toggle="popover" data-bs-html="true" data-bs-title="Explore Further" data-bs-content="<a href='/Sonic-Unreal/the-sonic-unreals-floating-signifiers'>The Sonic Unreal's Floating Signifiers</a>" style="text-decoration: underline; font-weight: bold;">given its slipperiness as a signifier and the fact that this slipperiness is what makes it a potent political tool</a>; any attempt to render (un)reality definable and static fundamentally stymies the very generativity in which we are interested in the first place.</p>
    <p>As such, this project seeks to track the forms and uses of (un)reality through its second-order effects. These second-order effects are many, but “The Sonic Unreal” focuses in on three: <a class="popup-hyperlink" data-bs-toggle="popover" data-bs-html="true" data-bs-title="Explore Further" data-bs-content="<a href='/Sonic-Unreal/virtuality'>Virtuality</a>" style="text-decoration: underline; font-weight: bold;"><em>virtuality</em></a>, <a class="popup-hyperlink" data-bs-toggle="popover" data-bs-html="true" data-bs-title="Explore Further" data-bs-content="<a href='/Sonic-Unreal/liveness'>Liveness</a>" style="text-decoration: underline; font-weight: bold;"><em>liveness</em></a>, and <a class="popup-hyperlink" data-bs-toggle="popover" data-bs-html="true" data-bs-title="Explore Further" data-bs-content="<a href='/Sonic-Unreal/immersion'>Immersion</a>" style="text-decoration: underline; font-weight: bold;"><em>immersion</em></a>. Each of these concepts names a malleable and contested aspect of reality, and through their deployment we can better understand the foundations they share.</p>
    <p>I am asking you, as the reader, to travel with me through this project, trusting that by sitting in the perhaps-uncomfortable space of uncovering (un)reality by attending to its echoes, its second-order effects, its ripples out into the world, we will come to understand it better than by naming it. Like an illusion (for, in many ways, <a class="popup-hyperlink" data-bs-toggle="popover" data-bs-html="true" data-bs-title="Explore Further" data-bs-content="<a href='/Sonic-Unreal/disco-ball-magic-lantern'>The Disco Ball & The Magic Lantern</a>" style="text-decoration: underline; font-weight: bold;">it is an illusion</a>), it is distorted through direct examination and takes on its full form only when examined askance.</p>
