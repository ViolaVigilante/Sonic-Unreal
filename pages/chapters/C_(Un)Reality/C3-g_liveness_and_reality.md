    <p>Liveness - as an <a class="popup-hyperlink" data-bs-toggle="popover" data-bs-html="true" data-bs-title="Explore Further" data-bs-content="<a href='/Sonic-Unreal/affect-liveness'>The Affect of Liveness</a>" style="text-decoration: underline; font-weight: bold;">affective orientation</a> and a <a class="popup-hyperlink" data-bs-toggle="popover" data-bs-html="true" data-bs-title="Explore Further" data-bs-content="<a href='/Sonic-Unreal/performativity-liveness'>The Performativity of Liveness</a>" style="text-decoration: underline; font-weight: bold;">performative aesthetic</a> - is a core mechanism by which reality becomes <a class="popup-hyperlink" data-bs-toggle="popover" data-bs-html="true" data-bs-title="Explore Further" data-bs-content="<a href='/Sonic-Unreal/texture-liveness'>The Texture of Liveness</a>" style="text-decoration: underline; font-weight: bold;">sensorially recognizable</a> as such. Many conceptions of reality are based on ideas of shared-ness, that the world you occupy and perceive is shared with others who are occupying and perceiving it in similar ways. Liveness might be <a class="popup-hyperlink" data-bs-toggle="popover" data-bs-html="true" data-bs-title="Explore Further" data-bs-content="<a href='/Sonic-Unreal/liveness'>Liveness</a>" style="text-decoration: underline; font-weight: bold;">most basically defined</a> as the sense of shared space and time and, thus, liveness becomes a common mode through which reality is delineated.</p>
    <p><a class="popup-hyperlink" data-bs-toggle="popover" data-bs-html="true" data-bs-title="Explore Further" data-bs-content="<a href='/Sonic-Unreal/liveness-television'>Liveness & Television</a>" style="text-decoration: underline; font-weight: bold;">Decades of scholarship on television broadcasting</a> have demonstrated the centrality of liveness (defined for those purposes as people sharing an experience of an event in “real” time in a variety of locations, an establishing of shared temporality even at geographic distance) not only to television as a medium but to <a class="popup-hyperlink" data-bs-toggle="popover" data-bs-html="true" data-bs-title="Explore Further" data-bs-content="<a href='/Sonic-Unreal/social-reality'>Social Reality</a>" style="text-decoration: underline; font-weight: bold;">the social orders which television inculcates</a>.
