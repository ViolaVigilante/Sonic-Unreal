    <p>The three main foci of this project - <a class="popup-hyperlink" data-bs-toggle="popover" data-bs-html="true" data-bs-title="Explore Further" data-bs-content="<a href='/Sonic-Unreal/esports-and-music'>Esports & Music</a>" style="text-decoration: underline; font-weight: bold;">music in <em>League of Legends esports</em></a>, <a class="popup-hyperlink" data-bs-toggle="popover" data-bs-html="true" data-bs-title="Explore Further" data-bs-content="<a href='/Sonic-Unreal/what_makes_virtual_concerts'>What Makes Virtual Concerts Virtual?</a>" style="text-decoration: underline; font-weight: bold;">live concerts in virtual worlds</a>, and <a class="popup-hyperlink" data-bs-toggle="popover" data-bs-html="true" data-bs-title="Explore Further" data-bs-content="<a href='/Sonic-Unreal/gaysian-nightlife-la'>Gaysian Nightlife in Los Angeles</a>" style="text-decoration: underline; font-weight: bold;">queer Asian American nightlife in Los Angeles</a> - map, respectively, onto AR, VR, and IRL.</p>
    <div class="alert alert-secondary" role="alert" style="text-align: center; margin: 0 12% 0 12%">
        <b>Augmented reality</b> (or <b>AR</b>) refers to media which alter a user’s perception of the world around them. Made popular through games such as <em>Pokémon GO</em>, where users look through their phone camera and see fictional creatures in the world around them, AR seeks to bring virtual phenomena into existence in the IRL world rather than creating a new, separate world. <a class="popup-hyperlink" data-bs-toggle="popover" data-bs-html="true" data-bs-title="Explore Further" data-bs-content="<a href='/Sonic-Unreal/augmented-reality'>Augmented Reality</a>" style="text-decoration: underline; font-weight: bold;">For more on this term, click here.</a>
    </div>
    <br>
    <div class="alert alert-secondary" role="alert" style="text-align: center; margin: 0 12% 0 12%">
        <b>Virtual reality</b> (or <b>VR</b>) refers to <a class="popup-hyperlink" data-bs-toggle="popover" data-bs-html="true" data-bs-title="Explore Further" data-bs-content="<a href='/Sonic-Unreal/immersion'>Immersion</a>" style="text-decoration: underline; font-weight: bold;">“immersive”</a> media which seek to isolate the senses of those interacting with them - where AR functions as a translucent screen through which the world is still perceivable, in VR that screen turns to an opaque wall, blocking out all else. The virtual world, in the ideal form of VR, occludes and replaces the IRL world. <a class="popup-hyperlink" data-bs-toggle="popover" data-bs-html="true" data-bs-title="Explore Further" data-bs-content="<a href='/Sonic-Unreal/virtuality-and-vr'>Virtuality & VR</a>" style="text-decoration: underline; font-weight: bold;">For more on this term, click here.</a>
    </div>
    <br>
    <div class="alert alert-secondary" role="alert" style="text-align: center; margin: 0 12% 0 12%">
        <b>IRL</b> is a term common in many online communities to refer to offline activities. Originally an acronym for “in real life,” the term today typically refers to an online/offline or on-computer/off-computer divide, without any assumption about which is more “real” than the other. <a class="popup-hyperlink" data-bs-toggle="popover" data-bs-html="true" data-bs-title="Explore Further" data-bs-content="<a href='/Sonic-Unreal/irl-or-afk'>IRL or AFK?</a>" style="text-decoration: underline; font-weight: bold;">For more on this term, click here.</a>
    </div>
    <br>
    <p>AR and VR are often used in conjunction with specific media technologies, becoming overdetermined by specific material interfaces rather than their formal structures or cultural use. VR is most commonly associated with <a class="popup-hyperlink" data-bs-toggle="popover" data-bs-html="true" data-bs-title="Explore Further" data-bs-content="<a href='/Sonic-Unreal/virtual-reality-concerts'>Virtual Reality Concerts</a>" style="text-decoration: underline; font-weight: bold;">virtual reality headsets</a> that users wear on their head to encompass their entire field of vision, for example, such that saying that one is going to do something “in VR” conjures such a headset without needing to be named outright.</p>

In this project, AR and VR are <a class="popup-comment" data-bs-toggle="popover" data-bs-html="true" data-bs-title='Ocularcentrism' data-bs-content='This takes shape in relation to a larger project of identifying and combating ocularcentrism in Western philosophy, theory, and culture writ large. We will take the augmented and the virtual, furthermore, not as mutually exclusive modes but rather as opposite poles on a fluid spectrum, a spectrum that can bend and shift and fold in upon itself such that VR and AR <a class="popup-hyperlink" data-bs-toggle="popover" data-bs-html="true" data-bs-title="Explore Further" data-bs-content="<a href='/Sonic-Unreal/augmenting-virtual-reality'>Augmenting Virtual Reality</a>" style="text-decoration: underline; font-weight: bold;">become nested inside one another</a>.</p>
    <p>By aligning <a class="popup-hyperlink" data-bs-toggle="popover" data-bs-html="true" data-bs-title="Explore Further" data-bs-content="<a href='/Sonic-Unreal/esports-augmented-reality'>Esports as Augmented Reality</a>" style="text-decoration: underline; font-weight: bold;">esports with AR</a>, <a class="popup-hyperlink" data-bs-toggle="popover" data-bs-html="true" data-bs-title="Explore Further" data-bs-content="<a href='/Sonic-Unreal/virtual-worlds'>Virtual Worlds</a>" style="text-decoration: underline; font-weight: bold;">virtual worlds with VR</a>, and <a class="popup-hyperlink" data-bs-toggle="popover" data-bs-html="true" data-bs-title="Explore Further" data-bs-content="<a href='/Sonic-Unreal/nightlife'>Nightlife</a>" style="text-decoration: underline; font-weight: bold;">nightlife with the IRL</a>, this project offers a mechanism for interrogating these categories and their attendant discourses and politics in realms far beyond these specific cases. We will slip back and forth between scales, considering <a class="popup-hyperlink" data-bs-toggle="popover" data-bs-html="true" data-bs-title="Explore Further" data-bs-content="<a href='/Sonic-Unreal/esports-and-nightlife'>Esports & Nightlife</a>" style="text-decoration: underline; font-weight: bold;">the overlapping spaces between nightlife and esports</a> at the same time as we attend to <a class="popup-hyperlink" data-bs-toggle="popover" data-bs-html="true" data-bs-title="Explore Further" data-bs-content="<a href='/Sonic-Unreal/esports-and-geopolitics'>Esports & Geopolitics</a>" style="text-decoration: underline; font-weight: bold;">the material positioning of esports in the IRL</a> and the ways that <a class="popup-hyperlink" data-bs-toggle="popover" data-bs-html="true" data-bs-title="Explore Further" data-bs-content="<a href='/Sonic-Unreal/potentiality-of-nightlife'>The Potentiality of Nightlife</a>" style="text-decoration: underline; font-weight: bold;">nightlife is always already an augmentation of multiple realities upon one another</a>. VR, AR, and IRL will prove to be as slippery in meaning and use as esports, virtual worlds, and nightlife themselves - <a class="popup-hyperlink" data-bs-toggle="popover" data-bs-html="true" data-bs-title="Explore Further" data-bs-content="<a href='/Sonic-Unreal/the-sonic-unreals-floating-signifiers'>The Sonic Unreal's Floating Signifiers</a>" style="text-decoration: underline; font-weight: bold;">floating signifiers</a> which derive use value from their definitional blurriness.</p>
