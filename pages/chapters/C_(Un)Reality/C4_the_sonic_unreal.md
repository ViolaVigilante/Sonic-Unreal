        <blockquote style="margin: 0;">
            <p>What if it were possible to vibrate at the same speed and velocity as our favorite music, our favorite sounds?</p>
            <footer style="padding-bottom: 1.5%;"><a class="epigraph_source" target="_blank" href="https://www.dukeupress.edu/the-lonely-letters">Ashon T. Crawley, <em>The Lonely Letters</em></a></footer>

    <p>	At the center of this project is its theory of <em>the sonic unreal</em>. In arguing that <a class="popup-hyperlink" data-bs-toggle="popover" data-bs-html="true" data-bs-title="Explore Further" data-bs-content="<a href='/Sonic-Unreal/unreality'>(Un)Reality</a>" style="text-decoration: underline; font-weight: bold;">the unreal is dialectically related to, rather than a passively produced externality of, the real</a>, <em>the sonic unreal</em> offers a theoretical framework for understanding how sound operates as a core mechanism for traversing this dialectic. <b><em>The sonic unreal</em> is a hermeneutic for analyzing how sound, music, and performance operate to establish and contest the always-contingent realms of reality and unreality. It demonstrates how sound (as music, as performance, and as vibrational energy) is a core mechanism through which the un/real dialectic is negotiated.</b></p>
    <p>	While sound is of course not the only hinge between reality and unreality, the sonic unreal shows how it bears an outsized power due to both its physical and cultural characteristics. <a class="popup-hyperlink" data-bs-toggle="popover" data-bs-html="true" data-bs-title="Explore Further" data-bs-content="<a href='/Sonic-Unreal/music-and-virtuality'>Music & Virtuality</a>" style="text-decoration: underline; font-weight: bold;">Music</a> and <a class="popup-hyperlink" data-bs-toggle="popover" data-bs-html="true" data-bs-title="Explore Further" data-bs-content="<a href='/Sonic-Unreal/sound-and-virtuality'>Sound & Virtuality</a>" style="text-decoration: underline; font-weight: bold;">sound</a> (along with various modes of engaging with them, from <a class="popup-hyperlink" data-bs-toggle="popover" data-bs-html="true" data-bs-title="Explore Further" data-bs-content="<a href='/Sonic-Unreal/performance-and-virtuality'>Performance & Virtuality</a>" style="text-decoration: underline; font-weight: bold;">performance</a> to <a class="popup-hyperlink" data-bs-toggle="popover" data-bs-html="true" data-bs-title="Explore Further" data-bs-content="<a href='/Sonic-Unreal/virtuality-of-play'>The Virtuality of Play</a>" style="text-decoration: underline; font-weight: bold;">play</a>) have each long been understood as originating from or operating within the unreal across historical and cultural contexts. Music not only appears unreal through its apparent immateriality and semantic indeterminacy; it also <a class="popup-hyperlink" data-bs-toggle="popover" data-bs-html="true" data-bs-title="Explore Further" data-bs-content="<a href='/Sonic-Unreal/sound-and-affect'>Sound & Affect</a>" style="text-decoration: underline; font-weight: bold;">operates vibrationally at a level which often appears prior to or outside of cognition</a>, appearing to bypass common modes of adjudicating reality through <a class="popup-hyperlink" data-bs-toggle="popover" data-bs-html="true" data-bs-title="Explore Further" data-bs-content="<a href='/Sonic-Unreal/reality-and-truth'>Reality & Truth</a>" style="text-decoration: underline; font-weight: bold;">truth claims</a> or <a class="popup-hyperlink" data-bs-toggle="popover" data-bs-html="true" data-bs-title="Explore Further" data-bs-content="<a href='/Sonic-Unreal/social-reality'>Social Reality</a>" style="text-decoration: underline; font-weight: bold;">social norms</a> (though, of course, social reality is based in <a class="popup-hyperlink" data-bs-toggle="popover" data-bs-html="true" data-bs-title="Explore Further" data-bs-content="<a href='/Sonic-Unreal/affective-reality'>Affective Reality</a>" style="text-decoration: underline; font-weight: bold;">embodied experience</a> as well).</p>
    <p>	This project analyzes (un)reality through <a class="popup-hyperlink" data-bs-toggle="popover" data-bs-html="true" data-bs-title="Explore Further" data-bs-content="<a href='/Sonic-Unreal/shared-foundations-virtuality-liveness-immersion'>The Shared Foundations of Virtuality, Liveness, and Immersion</a>" style="text-decoration: underline; font-weight: bold;">its second-order effects</a> of <a class="popup-hyperlink" data-bs-toggle="popover" data-bs-html="true" data-bs-title="Explore Further" data-bs-content="<a href='/Sonic-Unreal/liveness'>Liveness</a>" style="text-decoration: underline; font-weight: bold;"><em>liveness</em></a>, <a class="popup-hyperlink" data-bs-toggle="popover" data-bs-html="true" data-bs-title="Explore Further" data-bs-content="<a href='/Sonic-Unreal/virtuality'>Virtuality</a>" style="text-decoration: underline; font-weight: bold;"><em>virtuality</em></a>, and <a class="popup-hyperlink" data-bs-toggle="popover" data-bs-html="true" data-bs-title="Explore Further" data-bs-content="<a href='/Sonic-Unreal/immersion'>Immersion</a>" style="text-decoration: underline; font-weight: bold;"><em>immersion</em></a>. Each of these three categories of <a class="popup-hyperlink" data-bs-toggle="popover" data-bs-html="true" data-bs-title="Explore Further" data-bs-content="<a href='/Sonic-Unreal/liveness-reality'>Liveness & Reality</a>" style="text-decoration: underline; font-weight: bold;">liveness</a>, <a class="popup-hyperlink" data-bs-toggle="popover" data-bs-html="true" data-bs-title="Explore Further" data-bs-content="<a href='/Sonic-Unreal/virtuality-reality'>Virtuality & Reality</a>" style="text-decoration: underline; font-weight: bold;">virtuality</a>, and <a class="popup-hyperlink" data-bs-toggle="popover" data-bs-html="true" data-bs-title="Explore Further" data-bs-content="<a href='/Sonic-Unreal/immersion-reality'>Immersion & Reality</a>" style="text-decoration: underline; font-weight: bold;">immersion</a> shares a complex deterministic relationship with (un)reality, operating as metrics by which the reality of a phenomenon is assessed. Sound serves as a key factor in each of these discourses, from <a class="popup-hyperlink" data-bs-toggle="popover" data-bs-html="true" data-bs-title="Explore Further" data-bs-content="<a href='/Sonic-Unreal/musical-worlding'>Musical Worlding</a>" style="text-decoration: underline; font-weight: bold;">musical performance as a primary mode for articulating social liveness</a> to <a class="popup-hyperlink" data-bs-toggle="popover" data-bs-html="true" data-bs-title="Explore Further" data-bs-content="<a href='/Sonic-Unreal/edm-and-immersion'>EDM & Immersion</a>" style="text-decoration: underline; font-weight: bold;">sound’s role in communicating immersion by nature of its three-dimensional spatiality</a>.</p>
    <p>	“The Sonic Unreal” takes minoritarian life and aesthetics as its conceptual basis. <a class="popup-hyperlink" data-bs-toggle="popover" data-bs-html="true" data-bs-title="Explore Further" data-bs-content="<a href='/Sonic-Unreal/minoritarian-virtuality'>Minoritarian Virtuality</a>" style="text-decoration: underline; font-weight: bold;">Queer, trans, and racialized communities always already exist outside the realm of the majoritarian real</a>, and the performances of these communities (both mundane and spectacular) offer an aesthetics and politics of the unreal that must undergird any attempt to understand the un/real dialectic. By centering queer and racialized aesthetics, “The Sonic Unreal” emphasizes the contingency and multiplicity of (un)realities and demands attention to their effects on minoritarian life. Reality and unreality, here, are not abstract philosophical categories, but rather <a class="popup-hyperlink" data-bs-toggle="popover" data-bs-html="true" data-bs-title="Explore Further" data-bs-content="<a href='/Sonic-Unreal/texture-liveness'>The Texture of Liveness</a>" style="text-decoration: underline; font-weight: bold;">textures</a> of the lived world that have concrete impacts  disproportionately impacting precariously positioned communities within it. As a conceptual framework, <em>the sonic unreal</em> offers a theory of media, performance, and social life that aspires to broad applicability while keeping lived minoritarian experience at its core.</p>
