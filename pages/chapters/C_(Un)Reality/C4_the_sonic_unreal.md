    <p>	At the center of this project is its theory of <em>the sonic unreal</em>. In arguing that <a class="popup-hyperlink" data-bs-toggle="popover" data-bs-html="true" data-bs-title="Explore Further" data-bs-content="<a href='/Sonic-Unreal/unreality'>(Un)Reality</a>" style="text-decoration: underline; font-weight: bold;">the unreal is dialectically related to, rather than a passively produced externality of, the real</a>, <em>the sonic unreal</em> offers a theoretical framework for understanding how sound operates as a core mechanism for traversing this dialectic. <b><em>The sonic unreal</em> is a hermeneutic for analyzing how sound, music, and performance operate to establish and contest the always-contingent realms of reality and unreality. It demonstrates how sound (as music, as performance, and as vibrational energy) is a core mechanism through which the un/real dialectic is negotiated.</b></p>
