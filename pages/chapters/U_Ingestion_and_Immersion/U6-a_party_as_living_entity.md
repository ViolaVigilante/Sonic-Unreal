    <div class="epigraph">
        <blockquote style="margin: 0;">
            <p>The party: an organic entity, a living, breathing being, a gathering together of the multiple in one, an obscure order, a whole which is not one, a many that is singular, a kind of provisional ‘we’ at difference with itself from the inside out.</p>
            <footer style="padding-bottom: 1.5%;"><a class="popup-citation" onmouseover="myFunction(event)" onmouseout="myFunction(event)" href="https://nyupress.org/9781479832774/after-the-party/" target="_blank">Joshua Chambers-Letson, <em>After the Party: A Manifesto for Queer of Color Life</em><span class="popuptext">Chambers-Letson, Joshua. 2018. <em>After the Party: A Manifesto for Queer of Color Life</em>. New York: New York University Press. xi.</span></a></footer>
        </blockquote>
    </div>
    <p> Seeking life - <a class="popup-comment" data-bs-toggle="popover" data-bs-html="true" data-bs-title='More Life' data-bs-content='Chambers-Letson returns throughout <a target="_blank" href="https://nyupress.org/9781479832774/after-the-party/"><em>After the Party</em></a> to the idea of “More Life” as a crucial component of queer of color flourishing - but, critically, he leaves the term undefined except by its repeated use in the phrase “freedom and More Life” and the implication that it can only ever exist as a kind of Muñozian receding horizon.'>More Life<sup><b>&#8224;</b></sup></a> - in the wake of his mentor José Esteban Muñoz’s death, Joshua Chambers-Letson turned to the party as a site of living critical praxis. Theorizing the party as a <a class="popup-citation" onmouseover="myFunction(event)" onmouseout="myFunction(event)" href="https://nyupress.org/9781479832774/after-the-party/" target="_blank">“commons of incommensurability,”&#9873;<span class="popuptext">Chambers-Letson, Joshua. 2018. <em>After the Party: A Manifesto for Queer of Color Life</em>. New York: New York University Press. xx.</span></a> Chambers-Letson pushes back against discourses of <a class="popup-hyperlink" data-bs-toggle="popover" data-bs-html="true" data-bs-title="Explore Further" data-bs-content="<a href='/Sonic-Unreal/immersion-in-nightlife'>Immersion in Nightlife</a>" style="text-decoration: underline;"><b>nightlife immersion</b></a> in which <a class="popup-hyperlink" data-bs-toggle="popover" data-bs-html="true" data-bs-title="Explore Further" data-bs-content="<a href='/Sonic-Unreal/incommensurable-gaysianness'>Incommensurable Gaysianness</a>" style="text-decoration: underline;"><b>particularity dissolves into an undifferentiated mass</b></a>. Immersion is too often homogenization, assimilation, normalization; this dominant framework is not itself enough to enable liberation, <a class="popup-citation" onmouseover="myFunction(event)" onmouseout="myFunction(event)" href="https://press.umich.edu/Books/I/Ishtyle" target="_blank">as Kareem Khubchandani reminds us.&#9873;<span class="popuptext">Khubchandani, Kareem. 2020. <em>Ishtyle: Accenting Gay Indian Nightlife</em>. Ann Arbor, MI: University of Michigan Press. 7.</span></a> We might draw on Muñoz’s theory of <a class="popup-hyperlink" data-bs-toggle="popover" data-bs-html="true" data-bs-title="Explore Further" data-bs-content="<a href='/Sonic-Unreal/burden-of-liveness'>The Burden of Liveness</a>" style="text-decoration: underline;"><b>the burden of liveness</b></a> to ask who bears the burden of immersion, how it sits differently upon different bodies with different positionalities – and, how one might generatively disidentify with it.</p>
    <p>Let us take Chambers-Letson quite literally and consider the party “an organic entity, a living, breathing being.” Such a framing demands a reconsideration of what we mean by <a class="popup-hyperlink" data-bs-toggle="popover" data-bs-html="true" data-bs-title="Explore Further" data-bs-content="<a href='/Sonic-Unreal/liveness'>Liveness</a>" style="text-decoration: underline;"><b>“liveness.”</b></a> When we <a class="popup-hyperlink" data-bs-toggle="popover" data-bs-html="true" data-bs-title="Explore Further" data-bs-content="<a href='/Sonic-Unreal/liveness-and-the-body'>Liveness and the Body</a>" style="text-decoration: underline;"><b>return to liveness its connections with <em>life</em></b></a> - not just shared space and temporal synchronicity, but <a class="popup-hyperlink" data-bs-toggle="popover" data-bs-html="true" data-bs-title="Explore Further" data-bs-content="<a href='/Sonic-Unreal/liveness-and-as-animacy'>Liveness and/as Animacy</a>" style="text-decoration: underline;"><b>animacy</b></a> and vitality - what does it mean to say that a party is “live”? Not just live<em>ly</em>, but <a class="popup-hyperlink" data-bs-toggle="popover" data-bs-html="true" data-bs-title="Explore Further" data-bs-content="<a href='/Sonic-Unreal/liveness-and-as-aliveness'>Liveness and/as Aliveness</a>" style="text-decoration: underline;"><b><em>a</em>live</b></a>? Viewing the party as a living entity asks us to consider sorts of relationality we might enter into not only <em>through</em> it (as scholars such as <a class="popup-citation" onmouseover="myFunction(event)" onmouseout="myFunction(event)" href="https://www.dukeupress.edu/together-somehow" target="_blank">Luis-Manuel Garcia-Mispireta&#9873;<span class="popuptext">Garcia-Mispireta, Luis-Manuel. 2023. <em>Together, Somehow: Music, Affect, and Intimacy on the Dancefloor</em>. Durham, NC: Duke University Press.</span></a> and <a class="popup-citation" onmouseover="myFunction(event)" onmouseout="myFunction(event)" href="https://www.dukeupress.edu/feels-right" target="_blank">Kemi Adeyemi&#9873;<span class="popuptext">Adeyemi, Kemi. 2022. <em>Feels Right: Black Queer Women and the Politics of Partying in Chicago</em>. Durham, NC: Duke University Press.</span></a> have studied), but also <em>with</em> it? <a class="popup-hyperlink" data-bs-toggle="popover" data-bs-html="true" data-bs-title="Explore Further" data-bs-content="<a href='/Sonic-Unreal/hotpots-communal-soup'>HOTPOT's Communal Soup</a>" style="text-decoration: underline;"><b>How do we care for the party, sustaining its life?</b></a> <a class="popup-hyperlink" data-bs-toggle="popover" data-bs-html="true" data-bs-title="Explore Further" data-bs-content="<a href='/Sonic-Unreal/baby-gay'>Baby Gay</a>" style="text-decoration: underline;"><b>And what care does the party provide for us in return?</b></a></p>
    <p>This construction of the party establishes it as an animate and agential being, one which is capable of acting upon the world. What then are we to make of <a class="popup-hyperlink" data-bs-toggle="popover" data-bs-html="true" data-bs-title="Explore Further" data-bs-content="<a href='/Sonic-Unreal/immersion'>Immersion</a>" style="text-decoration: underline;"><b>immersion</b></a>? Viewing the party as agential asks us to focus on immersion as an act and a process, one which the party as agent enacts upon those subjects <a class="popup-hyperlink" data-bs-toggle="popover" data-bs-html="true" data-bs-title="Explore Further" data-bs-content="<a href='/Sonic-Unreal/immersion-and-agency'>Immersion & Agency</a>" style="text-decoration: underline;"><b>suspended</b></a> and <a class="popup-hyperlink" data-bs-toggle="popover" data-bs-html="true" data-bs-title="Explore Further" data-bs-content="<a href='/Sonic-Unreal/immersion-and-dissolution'>Immersion & Dissolution</a>" style="text-decoration: underline;"><b>dissolved</b></a> within it. Immersion here is a process of <a class="popup-hyperlink" data-bs-toggle="popover" data-bs-html="true" data-bs-title="Explore Further" data-bs-content="<a href='/Sonic-Unreal/metabolism'>Metabolism</a>" style="text-decoration: underline;"><b>metabolism</b></a>, of the party taking external matter and incorporating it into itself. When the party is “an organic entity,” <a class="popup-hyperlink" data-bs-toggle="popover" data-bs-html="true" data-bs-title="Explore Further" data-bs-content="<a href='/Sonic-Unreal/ingestion-immersion'>Ingestion & Immersion</a>" style="text-decoration: underline;"><b>immersion is ingestion</b></a>. <a class="popup-hyperlink" data-bs-toggle="popover" data-bs-html="true" data-bs-title="Explore Further" data-bs-content="<a href='/Sonic-Unreal/immersion-in-nightlife'>Immersion in Nightlife</a>" style="text-decoration: underline;"><b>Nightlife immersion</b></a> is an act of dual boundary crossing: <a class="popup-hyperlink" data-bs-toggle="popover" data-bs-html="true" data-bs-title="Explore Further" data-bs-content="<a href='/Sonic-Unreal/porousness-of-the-self'>The Porousness of the Self</a>" style="text-decoration: underline;"><b>of crossing into the party, and of the party crossing into you</b></a>. To be immersed is to be metabolized such that <a class="popup-hyperlink" data-bs-toggle="popover" data-bs-html="true" data-bs-title="Explore Further" data-bs-content="<a href='/Sonic-Unreal/hotpots-communal-soup'>HOTPOT's Communal Soup</a>" style="text-decoration: underline;"><b>your being provides sustenance for the party itself</b></a>.</p>
