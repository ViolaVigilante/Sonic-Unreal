    <p><i>Call of Duty</i> (<i>CoD</i>) is a first-person shooter first published by Activision in 2003. With a new edition of the game typically released every year (often in sub-franchises such as <i>Call of Duty: Modern Warfare</i> and <i>Call of Duty: Black Ops</i>), <i>CoD</i> is best known for its high-paced multiplayer game modes. <i>CoD</i> rose to prominence in the early days of YouTube, with players making names for themselves by posting gameplay montage videos and participating in “clans” such OpTic Gaming and FaZe Clan. </p>
    <p>Professional <i>Call of Duty</i> esports take place through the Call of Duty League (CDL). While smaller than major esports like <a class="popup-hyperlink" data-bs-toggle="popover" data-bs-html="true" data-bs-title="Explore Further" data-bs-content="<a href='/Sonic-Unreal/valorant'><i>Valorant</i></a>"><i>Valorant</i></a> and <a class="popup-hyperlink" data-bs-toggle="popover" data-bs-html="true" data-bs-title="Explore Further" data-bs-content="<a href='/Sonic-Unreal/league-of-legends'><i>League of Legends</i></a>"><i>League of Legends</i></a>, <i>CoD</i> esports has a long and vibrant history, including <a class="popup-hyperlink" data-bs-toggle="popover" data-bs-html="true" data-bs-title="Explore Further" data-bs-content="<a href='/Sonic-Unreal/history-of-esports-and-live-music'>The History of Esports & Live Music</a>">collaborations with musical artists such as Kanye West and Metallica</a>. A smaller esports scene also exists for <i>Call of Duty: Warzone</i>, a spinoff <a class="popup-hyperlink" data-bs-toggle="popover" data-bs-html="true" data-bs-title="Explore Further" data-bs-content="<a href='/Sonic-Unreal/apex-legends'><i>Apex Legends</i></a>">battle royale</a> game. Players regularly transfer between the <i>CDL</i> and the <i>Halo</i> esports scenes, given the games’ similar gameplay and overlapping <a class="popup-comment" data-bs-toggle="popover" data-bs-html="true" data-bs-title='<i>CoD</i> & <i>Fortnite</i>' data-bs-content='While less common, players also occasionally move between <i>CoD</i> and other esports such as <i>Fortnite</i>.'>playerbases.</a></p>
    <p>In line with first-person shooters as a genre more broadly, the <a class="popup-hyperlink" data-bs-toggle="popover" data-bs-html="true" data-bs-title="Explore Further" data-bs-content="<a href='/Sonic-Unreal/esports-and-race'>Esports & Race</a>">racial demographics</a> of the CDL are notably different (in terms of both players and fans) from those of professional esports in genres such as <a class="popup-hyperlink" data-bs-toggle="popover" data-bs-html="true" data-bs-title="Explore Further" data-bs-content="<a href='/Sonic-Unreal/league-of-legends'><i>League of Legends</i></a>">MOBAs</a> and <a class="popup-hyperlink" data-bs-toggle="popover" data-bs-html="true" data-bs-title="Explore Further" data-bs-content="<a href='/Sonic-Unreal/valorant'><i>Valorant</i></a>">tac shooters</a>. Where players in the LCS (the professional North American <a class="popup-hyperlink" data-bs-toggle="popover" data-bs-html="true" data-bs-title="Explore Further" data-bs-content="<a href='/Sonic-Unreal/league-of-legends'><i>League of Legends</i></a>"><i>League of Legends</i></a> league) are predominantly white and East Asian, for example, the CDL has a significantly higher number of Black players and fans and a significantly lower number of East Asian players and fans.</p>
    <br>
    <div class="alert alert-secondary" role="alert" style="text-align: center; margin: 0 12% 0 12%">
        <p><strong>First-Person Shooters (FPS)</strong> are a genre of video game characterized by high-paced, shooting-based gameplay in a first-person perspective. Some of the most influential early home video games such as <i>Doom</i> (1993) and <i>Quake</i> (1996) pioneered the FPS genre, and FPS games also helped lead the way in the development of early esports through events such as Quakecon (launched in 1996). FPS games range from historical military reenactments to science fiction epics, and - depending on the franchise - lean into both single-player and multiplayer gameplay.</p>
        <p>Examples of first-person shooters include <i>Call of Duty</i>, <i>Battlefield</i>, and <i>Halo</i>.</p>
