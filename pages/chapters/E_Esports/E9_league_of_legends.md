    <p><i>League of Legends</i> (often referred to as <i>League</i> or <i>LoL</i>) is a multiplayer online battle area (MOBA) where two teams of five try to destroy their opponents’ base while defending their own and competing for resources around the map. Launched in 2009 by Riot Games, it remains one of the world’s most-played video games. Runeterra, the fictional world in which <i>League</i> takes place, has become <a class="popup-hyperlink" data-bs-toggle="popover" data-bs-html="true" data-bs-title="Explore Further" data-bs-content="<a href='/Sonic-Unreal/transmedia-ecosystem-of-league-of-legends'>The Transmedia Ecosystem of <i>League of Legends</i></a>">a massive IP in its own right</a> - spawning media from <a class="popup-hyperlink" data-bs-toggle="popover" data-bs-html="true" data-bs-title="Explore Further" data-bs-content="<a href='/Sonic-Unreal/music-of-arcane'>The Music of <i>Arcane</i></a>">television shows</a> to comics to <a class="popup-hyperlink" data-bs-toggle="popover" data-bs-html="true" data-bs-title="Explore Further" data-bs-content="<a href='/Sonic-Unreal/teamfight-tactics'><i>Teamfight Tactics</i></a>">other spinoff video games</a>.</p>
    <br>
    <div class="alert alert-secondary" role="alert" style="text-align: center; margin: 0 12% 0 12%">
        <p><strong>Multiplayer Online Battle Arenas (MOBAs)</strong> are a genre of video game typically (though not always) characterized by a top-down or isometric perspective in which players on two teams rush to destroy the opposing team’s base while defending their own. MOBAs typically mix PVP (player-vs-player) and PVE (player-vs-enemy, referring to NPCs [non-player characters] or creatures controlled by the game’s AI) gameplay, with players “farming” the map by engaging in PVE to increase their characters’ strength and better combat the opposing team in PVP.</p>
        <p>Examples of MOBAs include <i>League of Legends</i>, <i>Dota 2</i>, and <i>Mobile Legends: Bang Bang</i>.</p>
    </div>
    <br>
    <p><i>League</i> is, by some metrics, the world’s largest esport. The game’s first esports competitions were held in 2011, two years after its release, and today there exist numerous professional, semi-professional, collegiate, and amateur leagues around the world. Its largest international tournaments have drawn almost seven million concurrent viewers and, according to one estimate, <a href="https://escharts.com/games/lol" target="_blank">over $125 million in prizes were awarded as of 2025.</a></p>
