    <p><i>Valorant</i> is a video game developed by Riot Games which combines elements from hero shooters and tactical shooters. Two teams of five players each take turns attacking and defending. <a class="popup-comment" data-bs-toggle="popover" data-bs-html="true" data-bs-title='<i>Counter-Strike</i>' data-bs-content='This gameplay loop is modeled after the popular <i>Counter-Strike</i> game franchise, where the teams are called “Terrorists” and “Counter-Terrorists” and the “spike” is referred to as a bomb. While <i>Valorant</i> did away with this language, the close equivalencies between the two games (with players, coaches, and commentators often switching between the two) mean that <i>Counter-Strike</i> language is still used in the context of <i>Valorant</i>.'>The goal</a> of the attacking team is to plant the “spike” in one of several designated areas and defend it from the the defending team until the timer expires; the goal of the defending team is to prevent the attacking team from accessing the areas in which the spike can be planted and/or defuse the spike after it has been planted.</p>
    <br>
    <div class="alert alert-secondary" role="alert" style="text-align: center; margin: 0 12% 0 12%">     
        <p><strong>Hero shooters</strong> are a genre of video game built on similar gun-based mechanics as traditional first- and third-person shooters such as <a class="popup-hyperlink" data-bs-toggle="popover" data-bs-html="true" data-bs-title="Explore Further" data-bs-content="<a href='/Sonic-Unreal/call-of-duty'><i>Call of Duty</i></a>"><i>Call of Duty</i></a>, mixed with unique character-based abilities. In traditional shooters gameplay is based entirely on movement and gunplay and every player has almost exactly the same abilities in-game (with slight variations based on, for example, what gun is being used), in hero shooters players choose specific characters with specific abilities to augment their weaponry. This makes gameplay more varied and tactical, though some players dislike these added mechanics for decreasing the importance of “raw” gun skill.</p>
        <p>Examples of hero shooters include <i>Valorant</i>, <a class="popup-hyperlink" data-bs-toggle="popover" data-bs-html="true" data-bs-title="Explore Further" data-bs-content="<a href='/Sonic-Unreal/apex-legends'><i>Apex Legends</i></a>"><i>Apex Legends</i></a>, and <i>Overwatch</i>.</p>
    </div>
    <br>
    <br>
    <div class="alert alert-secondary" role="alert" style="text-align: center; margin: 0 12% 0 12%">
        <p><strong>Tactical shooters</strong> (also sometimes called tac shooters) are a genre of video game which emphasize aim, strategic planning, team coordination, and (often) realist aesthetics. In contrast to popular high-paced, respawn-based shooters like <a class="popup-hyperlink" data-bs-toggle="popover" data-bs-html="true" data-bs-title="Explore Further" data-bs-content="<a href='/Sonic-Unreal/call-of-duty'><i>Call of Duty</i></a>"><i>Call of Duty</i></a> and <i>Battlefield</i> (where, typically, your character respawns when killed by an enemy character, and gameplay emphasizes speed and aim over tactics), tac shooters are typically much slower-paced and do not allow players to respawn while a game is ongoing.</p> 
        <p>Examples of tactical shooters include <i>Valorant</i>, <i>Counter-Strike</i>, and <i>Rainbow Six</i>.</p>
    </div>
    <br>
    <p><i>Valorant</i> is one of <a class="popup-comment" data-bs-toggle="popover" data-bs-html="true" data-bs-title="Riot Games' Esports" data-bs-content='Other Riot Games titles have competitive esports scenes on smaller scales, such as <i>Teamfight Tactics</i> and <i>Wild Rift</i>.'>Riot's two major esports titles</a>, along with <a class="popup-hyperlink" data-bs-toggle="popover" data-bs-html="true" data-bs-title="Explore Further" data-bs-content="<a href='/Sonic-Unreal/league-of-legends'><i>League of Legends</i></a>"><i>League of Legends</i></a>. While Valorant is a newcomer to the global esports scene in comparison to League of Legends, it has quickly become immensely popular with professional leagues around the world with <a href="https://escharts.com/games/valorant" target="_blank">viewership regularly reaching 1.5 million concurrent viewers for major international tournaments.</a></p>
    <p>While there is significant overlap between <a class="popup-hyperlink" data-bs-toggle="popover" data-bs-html="true" data-bs-title="Explore Further" data-bs-content="<a href='/Sonic-Unreal/league-of-legends'><i>League of Legends</i></a>"><i>League of Legends</i></a> and <i>Valorant</i> (many people are fans of both esports, for example, and many esports organizations field teams for both games), the esports have some significant differences. <i>Valorant</i> fans tend to skew younger and more racially diverse (at least in the US) than <i>League</i> fans, with a focus on hip hop aesthetics and competitive rivalries; <a class="popup-comment" data-bs-toggle="popover" data-bs-html="true" data-bs-title='Trash Talk on Stage' data-bs-content='This type of interaction between players on stage is shared across several shooter titles; it is particularly pronounced, for example, in <i>Call of Duty</i> esports.'>it is not unusual to see players on opposing teams stand up and yell at each other across the stage, for example, where this would be unheard of in <i>League</i>.</a> The music which accompanies the two esports (from <a class="popup-hyperlink" data-bs-toggle="popover" data-bs-html="true" data-bs-title="Explore Further" data-bs-content="<a href='/Sonic-Unreal/worlds-anthems'>Worlds Anthems</a>">anthems for and live performances at major tournaments</a> to the <a class="popup-hyperlink" data-bs-toggle="popover" data-bs-html="true" data-bs-title="Explore Further" data-bs-content="<a href='/Sonic-Unreal/silver-scrapes'>Silver Scrapes</a>">interstitial music that plays during broadcast breaks</a>) helps illuminate their divergences: League tends towards <a class="popup-hyperlink" data-bs-toggle="popover" data-bs-html="true" data-bs-title="Explore Further" data-bs-content="<a href='/Sonic-Unreal/silver-scrapes'>Silver Scrapes</a>">dubstep</a>, rock, and <a class="popup-hyperlink" data-bs-toggle="popover" data-bs-html="true" data-bs-title="Explore Further" data-bs-content="<a href='/Sonic-Unreal/kda'>K/DA</a>">K-pop</a>, while <i>Valorant</i> tends towards hip hop, drum ‘n’ bass, and reggaeton (though these are porous designations at best).</p>
