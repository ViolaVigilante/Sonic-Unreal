    <p><i>Apex Legends</i> is a multiplayer video game based on a battle royale gameplay loop with hero shooter elements. Twenty teams of three players each compete to be the last team standing on an ever-shrinking map; in contrast with traditional battle royales such as <i>PUBG</i>, where gameplay customization is based entirely on items found around the map, in <i>Apex</i> every player loads into the game as one of several possible distinct characters, each with a unique set of abilities separate from the items they may find during the game.</p>
    <br>
    <div class="alert alert-secondary" role="alert" style="text-align: center; margin: 0 12% 0 12%">
        <p><strong>Hero shooters</strong> are a genre of video game built on similar gun-based mechanics as traditional first- and third-person shooters such as <a class="popup-hyperlink" data-bs-toggle="popover" data-bs-html="true" data-bs-title="Explore Further" data-bs-content="<a href='/Sonic-Unreal/call-of-duty'><i>Call of Duty</i></a>"><i>Call of Duty</i></a>, mixed with unique character-based abilities. In traditional shooters gameplay is based entirely on movement and gunplay and every player has almost exactly the same abilities in-game (with slight variations based on, for example, what gun is being used), in hero shooters players choose specific characters with specific abilities to augment their weaponry. This makes gameplay more varied and tactical, though some players dislike these added mechanics for decreasing the importance of “raw” gun skill.</p>
        <p>Examples of hero shooters include <a class="popup-hyperlink" data-bs-toggle="popover" data-bs-html="true" data-bs-title="Explore Further" data-bs-content="<a href='/Sonic-Unreal/valorant'><i>Valorant</i></a>"><i>Valorant</i></a>, <i>Apex Legends</i>, and <i>Overwatch</i>.</p>
    </div>
    <br>
    <br>
    <div class="alert alert-secondary" role="alert" style="text-align: center; margin: 0 12% 0 12%">
        <p><strong>Battle royales</strong> (BRs) are a genre of multiplayer video game in which players compete to be the last person/team standing. BRs typically take place on a large map that gradually shrinks over time; players forage for weapons and items around the world, fighting with and/or hiding from one another to survive as long as possible as the playable area grows smaller and conflict with other players becomes increasingly frequent.</p>
        <p>Examples of battle royales include <i>Apex Legends</i>, <a class="popup-hyperlink" data-bs-toggle="popover" data-bs-html="true" data-bs-title="Explore Further" data-bs-content="<a href='/Sonic-Unreal/fortnite'><i>Fortnite</i></a>"><i>Fortnite</i></a>, and <i>PUBG: Battlegrounds</i>.</p>
    </div>
    <br>
    <p><i>Apex Legends</i> esports take place under the banner of the ALGS (Apex Legends Global Series), run by <i>Apex</i> publisher Electronic Arts and with professional leagues around the world. <i>Apex</i> is a smaller esport in terms of viewership and finances than major titles such as <a class="popup-hyperlink" data-bs-toggle="popover" data-bs-html="true" data-bs-title="Explore Further" data-bs-content="<a href='/Sonic-Unreal/league-of-legends'><i>League of Legends</i></a>"><i>League of Legends</i></a> and <a class="popup-hyperlink" data-bs-toggle="popover" data-bs-html="true" data-bs-title="Explore Further" data-bs-content="<a href='/Sonic-Unreal/valorant'><i>Valorant</i></a>"><i>Valorant</i></a>; unlike Tier-1 professional League, for example, <a class="popup-comment" data-bs-toggle="popover" data-bs-html="true" data-bs-title='Making a Living in <i>Apex Legends</i> Esports' data-bs-content='It is not uncommon for teams even at the highest level of international competition to lack sponsorship by an esports organization (commonly referred to simply as an “org”); when a team is supported by an org, salaries are often smaller and/or more temporary than more in larger esports.'>it is common for players to earn the majority of their income from streaming or an unrelated job rather than from team salaries.</a></p>
