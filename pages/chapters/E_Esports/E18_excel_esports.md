    <p>Microsoft Excel esports is exactly what it sounds like - a competitive, <a class="popup-hyperlink" data-bs-toggle="popover" data-bs-html="true" data-bs-title="Explore Further" data-bs-content="<a href='/Sonic-Unreal/gamification'>Gamification</a>">gamified</a> version of the Microsoft Excel spreadsheet software. In <a href="https://excel-esports.com/" target="_blank">the words of the Microsoft Excel World Championship organizers:</a></p>
    <div class="alert alert-light" role="alert">Excel Esports is a competition where participants solve unusual game tasks in Microsoft Excel. No finance, just Microsoft Excel and logical thinking skills. Excel Esports transforms a common office tool into a dynamic sport, bringing together a global community of enthusiasts to compete, learn, and celebrate the power of Excel.</div>
    <p>Excel Esports are quite small in comparison to major esports such as <a class="popup-hyperlink" data-bs-toggle="popover" data-bs-html="true" data-bs-title="Explore Further" data-bs-content="<a href='/Sonic-Unreal/league-of-legends'><i>League of Legends</i></a>"><i>League of Legends</i></a> - the 2026 Microsoft Excel Esports World Championships had a prize pool of $100,000, compared to the $5 million prize pool at the 2025 <i>League</i> world championships. Nevertheless, there is an avid playerbase and fan community for the esport that goes beyond those community outsiders with <a class="popup-hyperlink" data-bs-toggle="popover" data-bs-html="true" data-bs-title="Explore Further" data-bs-content="<a href='/Sonic-Unreal/the-affect-of-esports'>The Affect of Esports</a>">an ironic interest in the game’s novelty</a>.</p>
